Which products earn commissions?

Commissions apply to eligible Gangster Bougie products at the fixed rates shown in your affiliate dashboard. Some items are temporarily excluded because their production and delivery costs are higher. This keeps the program sustainable, and we review product eligibility periodically.

Items currently excluded from affiliate commissions:

  • Men’s basketball rib shorts (Onyx/Gold and Cream/Gold)
  • Crowned Luxury crew socks
  • Stainless steel gym bottles (12 oz and 18 oz)
  • Mini wrap bandanas

These products may still be purchased through your referral link, but they earn $0 commission. If an order includes other eligible items, you still earn the regular commission on those items.
